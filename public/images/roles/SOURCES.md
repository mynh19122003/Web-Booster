# Game role icon assets

- League of Legends role icons (`lol/*.svg`) are Riot client assets hosted by CommunityDragon: https://raw.communitydragon.org/10.13/plugins/rcp-fe-lol-leagues/global/default/svg/ . CommunityDragon notes these are Riot-owned assets and that it is not endorsed or sponsored by Riot Games.
- VALORANT role icons (`valorant/*.png`) are the in-game role icons exposed by the community-maintained Valorant API: https://valorant-api.com/v1/agents?isPlayableCharacter=true . Valorant-API is not endorsed by Riot Games; the icons are Riot intellectual property.
